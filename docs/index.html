Open-source Blender add-on

AI product rendering, grounded in your real 3D model.

Turn a Blender camera setup, a real product model, and environment reference images into polished product and automotive scenes.

Wondful combines Blender's composition and product masks with AI-assisted environment and lighting generation. Silhouette checks and detail restoration help keep the product aligned with the source model.

How it works

A camera-anchored workflow designed to reduce product drift during AI-assisted rendering.

Set up the model

Choose the product collection and compose the camera in Blender.

Add references

Provide product identity references and the target environment style.

Generate scenes

Use AI to develop the environment, atmosphere, and lighting.

Check and restore

Compare silhouettes and restore selected real details where supported.

Open source, with honest limits

The source code is released under the MIT License. AI image editing can still alter details; the workflow uses silhouette checks and correction steps to reduce these errors, but it does not guarantee pixel-perfect preservation for every generation.

For reproducible comparisons, see the case studies, which document prompts, references, settings, and silhouette measurements, including cases that did not meet the target.
